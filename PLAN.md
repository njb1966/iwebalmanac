# Independent Web Almanac (IWA)

## Vision

The Independent Web Almanac exists to advance and promote human-guided creative work through publishing, education, review, and thoughtful discussion.

We believe technology is most valuable when it amplifies human creativity rather than replacing it. As creative tools evolve, creators and readers need places that focus on craft, process, transparency, and outcomes rather than ideology or hype.

IWA is an editorial publication dedicated to exploring how people create meaningful work with modern tools.

---

## Mission

IWA helps readers discover thoughtful writing, publishing practices, and creative work at the intersection of human creativity and modern tools.

We publish fiction, essays, reviews, interviews, and educational resources that document and advance the craft of creating in the modern era.

---

## Core Philosophy

Publication first.

Institution later.

The path is:

**Publish → Earn Trust → Build Audience → Gain Influence → Shape Standards**

Authority is not declared.

Authority is earned through consistent editorial judgment and meaningful work.

---

## Editorial Position

IWA is neither pro-AI nor anti-AI.

We are interested in results.

The central question is:

> Did a human create something meaningful?

Not:

> How many words did the human type?

Technology matters.

Craft matters more.

---

## Editorial Leadership

The Independent Web Almanac is founded and edited by Nick Burchett.

Nick has spent decades working in technology, publishing online, building independent digital communities, and exploring creative workflows. He is the author of fiction and non-fiction works and an active practitioner of AI-assisted writing.

Editorial decisions are ultimately the responsibility of the editor and are guided by the mission and standards of the publication.

# Content Pillars

## Fiction

The creative heart of the publication.

IWA publishes selected fiction that demonstrates thoughtful human-guided creative work.

Possible formats:

* Short stories
* Serialized fiction
* Novella excerpts
* Experimental narrative projects

The goal is not volume.

The goal is quality.

Stories serve as examples of the creative practices discussed throughout the publication.

---

## Essays

Long-form editorial content exploring:

* Creativity
* Writing craft
* AI-assisted workflows
* Independent publishing
* Digital ownership
* Technology and culture
* The future of creative work

Focus on evergreen ideas rather than news cycles.

---

## Reviews

* One interview
