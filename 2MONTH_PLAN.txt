Independent Web Almanac Two-Month Plan
======================================

Timeframe: June 2026 through July 2026

Goal: prove the publication can publish consistently before adding more
infrastructure or workflows.


June 2026: Foundation and First Issue
-------------------------------------

Week 1: Site Readiness

- Review live site copy: Home, About, Editorial Philosophy, Follow.
- Tighten visual/layout issues after viewing on desktop and mobile.
- Add contact method to Follow page if ready.
- Confirm RSS feed URL and sitemap are correct.
- Add a short internal checklist for publishing each new piece.


Week 2: Editorial Pipeline

- Define working standards for each section:
  - Fiction: what qualifies for publication.
  - Essays: voice, length, topics.
  - Reviews: review criteria and structure.
  - Interviews: question format.
  - Resources: practical usefulness standard.
- Create reusable Markdown starter drafts for:
  - Essay.
  - Review.
  - Interview.
  - Fiction.
  - Resource.
- Draft a simple editorial calendar for June and July.


Week 3: First Content Batch

- Write and publish first essay.
- Write and publish first review.
- Draft second essay.
- Draft one resource article.
- Verify published pieces appear correctly on:
  - Homepage.
  - Section pages.
  - RSS.
  - Sitemap.


Week 4: First Issue Shape

- Publish second essay.
- Publish one resource article if ready.
- Decide whether the first fiction piece is ready or should wait.
- Review the homepage with real content and adjust hierarchy if needed.
- Note what felt slow or awkward in the publishing process.


July 2026: Cadence and Editorial Voice
--------------------------------------

Week 5: Review and Adjust

- Review first month's publishing workflow.
- Update HOWTO.txt if anything was unclear.
- Refine frontmatter conventions if needed.
- Decide whether "featured" should mean newest lead or editor-selected lead.


Week 6: Second Content Batch

- Draft and publish one essay.
- Draft and publish one review.
- Begin preparing first interview or interview format.
- Create a backlog of 6 to 10 article ideas across sections.


Week 7: Reader Infrastructure

- Improve Follow page with final RSS language.
- Add contact information if not already added.
- Decide whether newsletter stays future-only or gets a placeholder.
- Check live site from a feed reader.
- Confirm search engines can crawl the site.


Week 8: Month Two Close

- Publish second July essay.
- Publish interview if ready, otherwise queue it for August.
- Review whether monthly cadence is sustainable:
  - Two essays.
  - One review.
  - Quarterly interview.
  - Fiction and resources as available.
- Write a short internal retrospective:
  - What content was easiest to produce?
  - What took too long?
  - Which section feels strongest?
  - What should not be built yet?


Do Not Build Yet
----------------

- Submissions system.
- Contributor directory.
- Newsletter platform.
- Comments.
- Accounts.
- Forums or community features.
- Anthology infrastructure.
- Database-backed workflow.


Success By End Of July
----------------------

- Four essays published or drafted.
- Two reviews published or drafted.
- One resource article published or drafted.
- Interview format defined.
- RSS working.
- Publishing workflow documented.
- Site design tested with real content.
- Clear August editorial calendar.
